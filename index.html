Privacy Policy — Confession Booth Uploader

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Confession Booth Uploader is a small tool that uploads videos to the operator's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not a consumer app and is not offered to the general public.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account sign-in, solely to authorize uploads to your own YouTube channel.
  • The video files, titles, descriptions, and tags you choose to upload.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • It does not access your videos, channel, or account for any purpose other than the uploads you initiate.
  • It does not serve advertising or track you across other sites or apps.

Where your data is kept

Authorization tokens are stored locally in the operator's own environment so the tool can upload without signing in every time. No personal data is stored on a remote server operated by this app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
